# Business Model

## Revenue Strategy

### Pricing Philosophy

> **Core belief:** People will pay for privacy and ownership.

The market is full of subscription apps that hold your data hostage. We offer:
- **One-time purchase** (own it forever)
- **Your data stays yours** (no lock-in)
- **No recurring fees** (pay once, use forever)

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Free Tier
**Price:** $0

**Includes:**
- Statement import (unlimited)
- SQLite database (local storage)
- Basic CLI queries
- Manual categorization
- CSV export

**Limitations:**
- No AI assistant
- No auto-categorization
- No goals/budgets
- Community support only

**Purpose:** Let users try it, build trust, create word-of-mouth.

---

### Pro (One-Time Purchase)
**Price:** $29

**Includes everything in Free, plus:**
- 🤖 AI assistant (natural language queries)
- 🏷️ Auto-categorization
- 🎯 Goals & budget tracking
- 📊 Spending insights & trends
- 📈 Net worth tracking
- 🔄 Recurring transaction detection
- 📧 Email support

**Why $29?**
- Lower than 3 months of most finance apps
- High enough to signal quality
- One-time = no churn to worry about
- Comparable to a nice dinner out

---

### Business License
**Price:** $99

**For:** Financial advisors, accountants managing client data

**Includes everything in Pro, plus:**
- Multi-profile support (separate clients)
- Export reports (PDF)
- Priority support
- Custom parser requests (2/year)

## Why NOT Subscription?

| Subscription Model | One-Time Model |
|--------------------|----------------|
| Predictable recurring revenue | Larger upfront payment |
| Churn is a constant battle | No churn (already paid) |
| Users feel "rented" | Users feel ownership |
| Must justify value monthly | Value proven at purchase |
| Higher lifetime value (maybe) | Lower but guaranteed revenue |

**Our bet:** The privacy-conscious market values ownership. They'll pay more upfront to avoid subscriptions and data dependency.
